Cultural Homophily in Grand Slam Doubles

ATP Men's Grand Slam Doubles, 2018–2025  ·  1,793 matches after dropping 47 retirements / walkovers  ·  Regression sample: 3,222 team-obs (complete ranking, nationality/language, and GS experience for all 4 players)

1. Data and Measures

Three culture measures capture teammate similarity. Same nationality and same official language are binary (0/1). Language proximity is a continuous 0–1 index of ethnolinguistic closeness (higher = more similar language family).

Regressions use a team-level panel with tournament-by-year fixed effects, round fixed effects, and standard errors clustered by match. Controls in all main specifications: team average doubles ranking, opponent average ranking, top-100 singles indicator (either player), team average GS doubles appearances (exp_mean), and its square. Culture measures enter one at a time. Sensitivity tables (3b, 4b, 5b) additionally include teammate rank gap to verify stability; rank gap is insignificant throughout and is excluded from main specifications. Nationality and language data are fully resolved in the pipeline (birthplace fallback, surname lookup, Monaco language correction, and manual_nationality.csv); zero matches are dropped for nationality.
